# Dunking

## Approach

- 3–5 accelerating strides; final two steps are the jump setup (long penultimate,
  short plant — see jump_mechanics.md). Ball gathered during the penultimate step.
- One-foot dunks off speed (longer, flatter flight); two-foot power dunks off a
  jump-stop (vertical, strong in traffic).

## Ball carry

- One-hand: ball palmed/controlled against the forearm, swung up in one arc from
  hip to overhead during the rise.
- Two-hand: ball chinned at gather, driven straight up the midline; both elbows
  above rim level at the slam.
- The ball reaches its highest point *before* the slam — cocked at/behind the head
  at apex, then driven down. The cock-then-slam is the anticipation beat.

## The slam

- Wrist(s) snap down through the rim; contact with rim is common and intentional
  (grip prevents the ball bouncing back off the back rim).
- Body arches back slightly at apex, then crunches forward as the arms drive down —
  core does the slam as much as the arms.
- Eyes on rim from the gather until ball is through.

## After

- Rim grab: momentum management, not style — releases once the body swings under.
- Landing: two feet, deep knee absorption (dunk landings are the hardest in the
  game, 4×+ bodyweight), often a backward stumble-step that reads as power.

## Game application (Cyber Hoops)

- Current dunk: lunge + overhead carry + downward release — matches the two-hand
  template. Missing beats, in payoff order:
  1. **Cock-then-slam:** hold ball slightly behind head at apex (the +50% hang
     window), then fast slam — anticipation → payoff.
  2. **Back-arch at apex → forward crunch on slam** (spec K has it; not yet coded).
  3. Landing stumble-step + deep absorb after big dunks.
  4. Rim grab as a rare flourish on close-range dunks.
- One-foot vs two-foot by approach speed: sprint arrival = one-foot flatter lunge;
  slow arrival = two-foot vertical. Pure animation switch, same gameplay.
