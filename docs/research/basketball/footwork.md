# Footwork

## Stances

- **Athletic base:** feet shoulder-width+, knees 20–40°, weight on balls of feet,
  heels light. Every basketball action starts and ends here.
- **Triple threat (with ball, no dribble):** staggered stance, ball on hip, can
  shoot/pass/drive — the decision posture.

## Steps that matter

- **First step:** the separator. Long, low, past the defender's foot; body weight
  falls forward into it. Preceded by the pre-load dip (see biomechanics).
- **Jab step:** short (~20cm) sharp fake with the non-pivot foot, weight stays on
  pivot leg so any real move can follow instantly.
- **Drop step:** post move — one foot swings backward around the defender,
  hips seal them behind; power comes from the hip turn.
- **Pivots:** ball of the pivot foot grinds, never slides. Front pivot (chest
  leads) to face up; reverse pivot (back leads) to seal.
- **Gather (two-step rhythm):** on the move, ball is secured during step 1,
  step 2 is the launch step. All layups/dunks/pull-ups ride this 1-2 beat.

## Braking and cutting

- **Two-step stop:** penultimate step long (brake), final step short (stabilize).
- **Jump stop:** both feet land together off a low hop — instant legal pivot
  choice, reads "solid".
- **Cuts:** plant foot outside the new line, hips turn first, push off the plant.
  Sharpness of a cut = how far outside the line the plant lands.

## Defensive slides (see defense.md)

- Step-slide, never crossing feet; lead foot points the direction, trail foot
  pushes. Width maintained the whole slide.

## Game application (Cyber Hoops)

- Gait already distance-driven; two-step stop and plant-first cuts are the H and F
  entries in the animation spec.
- Foot IK plant-locking makes pivots/brakes read; sliding feet destroy them.
- Jab step = cheap future juke mechanic: needs only a 100ms one-shot + defender AI
  reaction hook.
- Gather rhythm gates layup/dunk animations — 1-2 beat before takeoff, not a
  standing jump.
