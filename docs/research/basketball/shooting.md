# Shooting Mechanics

## The kinetic chain

Power flows legs → hips → torso → shoulder → elbow → wrist → fingers. The arm aims;
the legs shoot. Elite shooters show near-identical leg drive on every shot — range
is added with legs, never with arm effort (arm-powered shots lose arc first).

## Form fundamentals (near-universal)

- **Base:** feet shoulder width, shooting-side foot a few cm ahead ("toe-heel
  relationship"), knees flexed pre-shot.
- **Grip:** ball on finger pads, air gap under the palm; guide hand on the side,
  contributing zero force.
- **Set point:** ball above the forehead, elbow under the ball, forearm vertical
  from the front view. One straight line: ball–elbow–knee–foot.
- **Release:** legs extend and the arm unfolds in one motion; wrist snaps down
  ("goose-neck" hold), ball leaves off index/middle fingers.
- **Follow-through:** arm stays extended ~1s. Not decoration — cutting it early
  changes the wrist snap and short-arms the shot.
- **Eyes:** on the rim (front or back edge) from gather to release; never follow
  the ball's flight until it's gone.

## Ball flight

- Optimal launch angle 45–52° from ~4–7m (higher when closer). Flat shots (<42°)
  shrink the effective rim opening dramatically.
- Backspin ~2–3 rev/s: stabilizes flight, kills bounce energy on rim contact
  ("shooter's roll"), makes rim hits die downward instead of ricocheting.
- Release height + jump adds arc room: release near jump apex for contested shots,
  on the way up for quick catch-and-shoot.

## Timing profile

- Catch-to-release for elite: 0.4–0.6s. Gather (dip) is part of the rhythm — the
  ball dips to waist even on catch-and-shoot; removing the dip breaks the chain.
- Jump shot: dip → rise → release just before/at apex → land same spot (balance
  check; drifting = rushed).

## Game application (Cyber Hoops)

- Shot arc solver already parameterizes apex clearance — maps to launch angle.
- Timing meter (future skill mechanic): release window keyed to jump apex mirrors
  the real apex-release rule; early/late = short/long miss, not random.
- Add backspin visual to shot ball; rim contact damping already arcade-tuned.
- Follow-through hold = the 60ms apex freeze in the arcade spec — same read.
