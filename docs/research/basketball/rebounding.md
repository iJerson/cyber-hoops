# Rebounding

## Where misses go

- ~70–75% of misses rebound to the side *opposite* the shot ("weak side rule").
- Long shots → long rebounds: three-point misses commonly bounce past the free-throw
  line; close misses die near the rim.
- Flat shots ricochet hard and far; high-arc shots with backspin drop soft and near.

## Box-out mechanics

- On the shot: find your man first, ball second. Reverse pivot into their path,
  forearm feel contact, hips low and wide, seal them behind.
- Hold the seal 1–2s (most rebounds are decided before the ball arrives), then
  release to the ball at its predicted landing spot.

## Timing the jump

- Jump to catch the ball at *your* apex, not the ball's first arrival height —
  mistimed early jumps are the common error.
- Two-hand catch, ball ripped to chin height ("chin it"), elbows out on landing.

## Second effort

- Tip mechanics: one-hand fingertip control when out of reach — direct to self or
  soft-tip at the rim.

## Game application (Cyber Hoops)

- Ball already rebounds physically off rim/backboard; miss distribution emerges
  from physics — verify long-shot misses carry farther (bounciness supports it).
- Rebound skill mechanic (future): predicted-landing indicator for AI (solvable
  analytically from ball velocity) and box-out as a body-collision stance that
  slows the opponent's path to the spot.
- Chin-it pose = pickup animation upgrade when the ball is caught airborne.
